# Hypotheses and competing explanations

[Project overview](README.md) · [Experiment log](experiment-log.md) · [Results](results.md)

These are candidate explanations considered within an exploratory discussion. None has been demonstrated for this jacket. This file is a hypothesis register, not a verified ingredient analysis, literature review, or cleaning protocol.

## Outcome at closure

The investigator reported weaker, now acceptable perfume odor and closed the experiment on 2026-11-05. She considered the cleaner helpful and suggested that fewer fragrance molecules remained. Molecule quantity was not measured; that explanation and the candidate mechanisms below remain unverified.

## Persistence

| ID | Candidate mechanism | What remains unknown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H01 | Some odor-producing material may be retained within garment layers or associated with fibers, limiting removal by surface cleaning. | The location of the residue, the shell and lining materials, and whether the insulation contains fragrance residue are unverified. |
| H02 | Some residual fragrance components may leave the garment slowly under the conditions encountered. | No remaining compounds, volatility values, concentrations, or release rates have been measured. Persistence alone cannot identify a compound or prove a mechanism. |
| H03 | Partial treatment may leave an untreated reservoir contributing to the perceived odor. | The original distribution and exact treated area were not mapped. This is a possible explanation, not an observed reservoir. |

## Removal or perceived improvement

| ID | Candidate explanation | Attribution limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H04 | Surface cleaning and outdoor airing may have removed or released some odor-producing material. | A reduction was reported after the earlier interventions, but their individual contributions and the role of elapsed time cannot be separated. |
| H05 | Cleaning action from the cleaner's overall formulation could have contributed to the reported improvement. Possible contributors include surfactants or solvents **if present**. | The actual formulation and ingredients have not been verified; no particular ingredient can be credited. |
| H06 | Moisture, redistribution of residue, ventilation, or additional elapsed time could affect a later odor assessment. | These conditions are not controlled or quantitatively recorded. A temporal association with cleaner use would not isolate its effect. |
| H07 | A biological component of the cleaner might contribute to modification of some odor-producing material. | The organisms, activity, substrates, conditions, and relevant fragrance compounds are unverified. There is no established evidence here of probiotic degradation of Seminalis fragrance compounds. |
| H08 | Cleaner scent or changes in assessment conditions could alter the perceived perfume odor without establishing removal. | Cleaner scent and assessment conditions have not been documented. No such masking effect has been observed in the current record. |

The investigator's previous success with this type of cleaner for pet-related household odors motivated H07. It does not establish that the same mechanism operates on perfume residue or that any biological component is responsible for household efficacy.

## Interpreting the completed case

- The reported reduction supports a descriptive statement about this jacket after this sequence of actions. It does not establish a specific degradation pathway.
- Drying state at the final assessment and persistence of the improvement were not documented. No sustained post-drying effect is claimed.
- Distinguishing biological action from formulation effects would require additional evidence and comparisons not present in this case. No such tests are claimed or committed to here.

No inference should be made from the fragrance's name about the chemical identity or biological origin of the residue.
