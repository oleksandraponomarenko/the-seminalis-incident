## Final reported outcome

In her final report, Oleksandra described the jacket's perfume odor as weaker and now acceptable to smell. She considered the cleaner helpful and chose to end the experiment. Complete odor elimination was not reported.

This is a subjective, qualitative practical outcome after partial cleaner treatment, water rinsing, and balcony drying/airing, following the earlier vinegar, surface-cleaning, and airing interventions. No percentage reduction or numerical odor score was supplied. The exact time and drying state of the final assessment, and persistence of the improvement over time, were not recorded.

## Reported outcome before the cleaner

Following partial treatment with diluted vinegar, limited surface cleaning, and subsequent overnight outdoor airing, Oleksandra reported that the odor had decreased but remained present.

This is a qualitative, firsthand assessment. No numerical reduction, instrumental measurement, or separate effect of vinegar, surface cleaning, and airing can be assigned.

## Interpretation boundary

The practical outcome was favorable in the investigator's assessment. Her attribution of benefit to the cleaner is recorded as her interpretation. This supports a descriptive conclusion about this jacket following the reported sequence; it does not establish general perfume-removal efficacy.

This uncontrolled sequence cannot isolate the cleaner's contribution from rinsing, ventilation, elapsed time, or prior treatments, or separate probiotic action from the cleaner's overall formulation. No chemical analysis established fewer fragrance molecules or a degradation pathway. The result describes one jacket under the reported circumstances.
